# Source provenance and redistribution

AoTT-8311 builds on the XGS-PON 8311 community firmware ecosystem and OpenWrt/LuCI. Existing source-level copyright, attribution, and licensing notices remain applicable.

Some source files originate from community firmware projects and may be subject to separate licenses. Their applicable licenses and redistribution terms must be checked individually before a public binary release or project-wide license grant.

The repository intentionally excludes proprietary vendor binaries, PON firmware, OMCI executables, kernel images, bootcore files, subscriber settings, credentials, and live device backups. It does not authorize redistribution of those components.

See `SECURITY.md` and `docs/BUILD.md` for privacy and build boundaries.
