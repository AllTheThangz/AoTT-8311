Activation gate

Install/activate is intentionally unavailable. Activation requires verified backup of the active certificate/key/config, an isolated uhttpd load test, a timed rollback path, a second local recovery path, and a real browser handshake to the intended management identity.

Operator-owned certificate import remains validation-only until equivalent native key/chain validation and rollback are integrated.
