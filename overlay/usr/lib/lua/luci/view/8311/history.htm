Local history

Optional, low-overhead local history for PON association, host Ethernet link state and measured host-interface throughput. History is RAM-only in this V3 stage. It does not claim ISP uptime, does not test the public Internet, and does not write periodic history to flash.

Collector settings

Disabled is the firmware default. Enabling starts one low-priority foreground worker with no automatic respawn loop.

Worker state: <%=history.service_running==true and "running" or history.service_running==false and "stopped" or "unknown"%>. Persistent history: not enabled in this stage.

Settings are read from this ONU. Service state is checked independently through the collector lock.
