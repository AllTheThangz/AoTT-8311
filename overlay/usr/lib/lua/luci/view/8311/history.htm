Local history

Optional, low-overhead local history for PON association, host Ethernet link, host-interface throughput, and opt-in optical/temperature readings. History is RAM-only in V3r4. It does not claim ISP uptime or test the public Internet, and it does not write periodic history to flash.

Collector settings

Disabled is the firmware default. Enabling starts one low-priority foreground worker with no automatic respawn loop.
