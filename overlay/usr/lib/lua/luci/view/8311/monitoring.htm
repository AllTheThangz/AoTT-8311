Monitoring & telemetry

Use your own syslog receiver or Prometheus-compatible collector. AoTT-8311 exposes bounded monitoring data, but does not store historic metrics or bundle a monitoring server.

Scope and security

All settings apply to this ONU only. Collector scheduling, storage, dashboards and notification rules are managed externally. Metrics require authenticated LuCI access and are disabled until explicitly enabled. Remote log reception must be confirmed on the receiving server.

Remote syslog forwarding

Uses the existing OpenWrt logging service. No additional daemon or persistent flash log history is installed.

Specify the receiver you operate. No destination is bundled or inferred.

The qualified logger supports UDP and TCP. Remote severity filtering is not supported by its forwarding path; filter log levels on your collector.
